CELLULOID SERVICES

Black Lab offers a range of professional celluloid services for film productions.

DEVELOPING

Black Lab offers professional film developing services to preserve your precious memories for a lifetime. Our experienced technicians ensure the highest quality results with fast turnaround times, handling every film with meticulous care and attention to detail.

PRINTING

Black Lab offers direct film printing from DCP, DPX, ProRes, and even MPEG4 files; no picture, sound, or subtitle negatives required, and no colour matching needed. We provide subtitles, security watermarking, and even 3D prints. Our prints are affordable, DCP-equivalent in price, deliver excellent quality, and come with super-fast turnaround times.

SCANNING

Achieve the authentic ‘film’ look by adding real grain and texture to your digital content, no effects or filters needed.

At Black Lab, we use state-of-the-art technology to record your digital content onto 35mm motion picture film and scan it back to digital, delivering genuine cinematic quality.
