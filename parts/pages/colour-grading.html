Colour grading is the lifeblood of any production, transforming the flat, grey, and washed-out look of LOG footage into vibrant, cinematic visuals.

At Black Lab, our colour grading services include precise colour correction and full grade transformations. With talented in-house colourists and state-of-the-art facilities, we bring your film’s look to life while preserving the drama and storytelling in every frame.

Proper grading ensures your audience connects with your story, whether it’s a high-budget feature or an indie production.
