Black Lab is a leading independent post-production company with studios in the UK, Mauritius, Malta, Germany, and the USA. We specialize in high-quality post-production, VFX, sound post-production, ADR, international deliverables, digital cinema mastering, cloud-based DIT, delivery, storage, and rushes management.

Our state-of-the-art facilities include in-house BARCO grading suites and screening rooms, fully compliant with 2K P3 / REC 709 DCI standards, ensuring your project meets the highest cinematic quality.

With multiple international locations, Black Lab provides seamless, end-to-end solutions for film, TV, and digital content. Contact us today to see how our expert team can elevate your next project.
