BLACK LAB – INTERNATIONAL POST-PRODUCTION, VFX, SOUND & FILM SERVICES

Black Lab is a leading independent post-production company with studios in the UK, Mauritius, Malta, Germany, and the USA. We specialize in high-quality post-production, VFX, sound post-production, ADR, international deliverables, digital cinema mastering, cloud-based DIT, delivery, storage, and rushes management.
